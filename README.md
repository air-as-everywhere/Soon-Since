# Soon & Since

A quiet place for the moments that matter.

Track how far you’ve come, look forward to what’s next, and remember the people you care about—with live counters, gentle animations, and a look that feels yours.

## Features

- **Time since & time until** — live counters down to seconds. Countdowns automatically become elapsed-time trackers when their date arrives.
- **Your featured moment** — choose a tracker to keep at the top.
- **People & occasions** — birthdays, anniversaries, and yearly reminders.
- **Personal appearance** — Soft White, Soft Black, and image-inspired palettes.
- **Shareable cards** — turn a moment into a photo card.
- **Helpful tools** — date differences, adding or subtracting days, time conversion, and a customizable weekend counter.
- **Year progress** — calendar-aware calculations, including leap years.
- **Backup & restore** — export your trackers and preferences to a JSON file.
- **Offline use** — everyday tracking works without an internet connection.

## Install on Android

**Requires Android 7.0 or later.**

1. Download the latest `.apk` file from **Releases**.
2. Open the file and allow installation from that source if Android asks.
3. Open **Soon & Since**.

Enable notifications for reminders. Precise timing is optional and may require an additional Android permission.

## Privacy

Your trackers and preferences stay on your device. No account is required, and the Android app does not request internet access.

Sharing a card, exporting a backup, or adding an event to your calendar happens when you choose to do so. Backup files contain readable personal data—store them somewhere safe.
